A Visual History of FAC

An introduction to the changing visual identity of First Alliance Church.

The way FAC has represented itself visually has changed across generations.

The 2026 FAC Brand Guidelines present logo designs associated with the 1980s, 2000s, 2011, 2016, and 2026. The current mark incorporates an architectural illustration of the church building alongside the First Alliance Church wordmark.

This is an introductory exhibit. To tell the fuller story, the archive will need original logo files, dated publications showing the marks in use, and contextual information from people involved in each redesign.

What we hope to preserve

Original designs, dated bulletins, photographs, printed materials, and recollections can help document how the church communicated across different periods.

Source: FAC Brand Guidelines (2026), pp. 8–12. Do not recreate or alter FAC logo artwork; use approved assets.
