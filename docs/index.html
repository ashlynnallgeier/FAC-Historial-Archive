Through the years

A history worth remembering.

From the church's early beginnings to the present day, explore how the FAC story has unfolded. Additional milestones will be added as historical sources are verified.

1913

Church incorporation

Incorporated as The Gospel Tabernacle of the Christian and Missionary Alliance.

1965

A new name

The church officially adopted the name First Alliance Church of Erie.

2026

A new visual identity

FAC's current brand identity introduces an architectural logomark inspired by the church building.

Milestones above are drawn from FAC's 2026 brand guidelines. Historical details and images can be expanded as materials are cataloged.
