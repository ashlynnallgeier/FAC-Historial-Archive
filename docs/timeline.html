Through the years

Historical Timeline

A growing chronology of milestones in FAC’s history. Only dates supported by available sources appear here.

1913

Church incorporation

The church was incorporated as The Gospel Tabernacle of the Christian and Missionary Alliance.

Source: FAC Brand Guidelines (2026), p. 7

October 6, 1965

First Alliance Church of Erie

The name was officially changed to The First Alliance Church of Erie, Pennsylvania of the Christian and Missionary Alliance.

Source: FAC Brand Guidelines (2026), p. 7

2026

Current visual identity

The brand guidelines document FAC’s current architectural logomark and wordmark.

Source: FAC Brand Guidelines (2026), pp. 8–10

Additional dates will be added when supporting documents can be verified. This is not yet a complete timeline.
