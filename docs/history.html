Our beginnings

More than 125 years of ministry.

For more than 125 years, First Alliance Church has shared the hope of Jesus in Erie and around the world. Generations of people have worshiped, served, prayed, discipled, and been sent from this church to participate in God’s mission.

FAC is part of the Christian and Missionary Alliance. Its current vision is to be a place to begin, belong, and believe, and its mission is to build, equip, and mobilize followers of Jesus.

This archive will help preserve historical records and first-person stories as the collection is reviewed and digitized.
