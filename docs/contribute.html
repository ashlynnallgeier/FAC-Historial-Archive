Help preserve the story

Contribute to the Archive

Historical materials and personal memories can help future generations understand FAC’s history.

What to share

Have something to contribute?

Old photographs, bulletins, newsletters, programs, correspondence, and memories may help fill gaps in the historical record.

Please contact the church office before dropping off or sending original materials. The archive team can discuss the item, ownership, permissions, and whether a digital copy may be appropriate.

Materials are reviewed before anything is published. Submitting an item does not automatically authorize public display.
